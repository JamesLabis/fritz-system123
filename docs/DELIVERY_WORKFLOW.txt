BUILDSMART — ORDER-TO-DELIVERY WORKFLOW

STEP BY STEP
1. Customer places an order            Status: Pending. Stock is reserved. Receipt and notification are created.
2. Admin reviews (Order Management)    Confirm, or Cancel with a reason.
   - Online Payment orders: open the order and use "Verify online payment" (enter the reference you checked
     in your bank / e-wallet). The order cannot be prepared until this is done.
3. Admin prepares                      Status: Preparing.
4. Admin assigns a driver              Delivery Management > To assign > Assign driver (driver + date + notes).
                                       Status: Ready for Delivery. Customer is notified of driver and date.
                                       (Edit driver/date any time while the delivery is still "Assigned".)
5. Admin dispatches                    Dispatch. Status: Out for Delivery. For Cash on Delivery the customer is told
                                       the amount to prepare.
6. Proof of delivery                   Mark delivered: enter who received it. For Cash on Delivery enter the cash
                                       collected (must equal the order total). Order becomes Completed and the COD
                                       payment becomes Paid.
   OR Delivery failed                  Failed: enter the reason. The order returns to "To assign" (Ready for Delivery)
                                       and can be rescheduled with the same or another driver. Stock stays reserved.
   Every attempt is kept (Order > Delivery table).

ALLOWED ORDER STATUS MOVES (enforced on the server)
  Pending            -> Confirmed, Cancelled
  Confirmed          -> Preparing, Cancelled
  Preparing          -> Ready for Delivery (by assigning a driver), Cancelled
  Ready for Delivery -> Out for Delivery (by dispatching), Cancelled
  Out for Delivery   -> Completed (by proof of delivery), Ready for Delivery (failed delivery), Cancelled
  Completed / Cancelled -> final
The Orders page only offers Confirmed, Preparing and Cancelled. The delivery steps are done in Delivery Management
because they need a driver, a date and proof.

CANCELLING
- Stock is returned exactly once. Any open delivery for the order is cancelled.
- Unpaid orders: payment becomes Failed.
- Already-paid orders (verified online payments): tick "Mark payment as refunded". Payment becomes Refunded.
  The system does not send money back; refund the customer yourself.

AUDIT LOG
Admin > Activity Logs records: logins and failed admin logins, order status changes, payment verification,
delivery assign / edit / dispatch / complete / failed, driver changes, stock changes, product create/update/delete,
settings and password changes, CSV exports.

NOT INCLUDED
Live GPS tracking, route optimisation, SMS/e-mail sending, a driver login or mobile app, supplier records and purchase
orders. Notifications are in-app only.
